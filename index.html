Privacy Policy — Plato Houston

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Plato Houston ("the app") lets you search and view public food-establishment health inspection records for Houston, Texas. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

Account email and password. To use the app you create an account with an email address and password. Authentication is provided by Supabase; your password is stored only as a secure hash and is never visible to us.

What we do not collect. The app does not collect your name, contacts, photos, files, precise location, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Inspection data

The inspection records shown in the app are public records published by the City of Houston health department. They are about businesses, not about you, and are not linked to your account.

How we use information

Your email is used only to sign you in and to operate your account (sign-in, password reset, account deletion). We do not send marketing email.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. Infrastructure providers (Supabase for sign-in and data, Apple/Google for app delivery) process data only to run their services under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can permanently delete your account inside the app at Account → Delete account. This removes your account and sign-in credentials. You can also stop using the app at any time.

Children

The app is a business-records reference tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/chemo77047/plato-houston or contact the app operator through the App Store listing.
